# USKE KHAT

[Intro – Soft Piano]

Aaj phir khole maine, woh purane se khat,
Kuch sookhe hue phool, kuch adhure jazbaat...
Teri likhi hui baatein, ab bhi zinda hain,
Bas tu hi nahi hai, jo kabhi kehti thi saath...

[Verse 1]

Almari ke kone mein, ek kitaab rakhi hai,
Teri yaadon ki maine, har baat rakhi hai,
Woh pehla sa khat tera, ab bhi sambhala hai,
Jispe tune likha tha, "Tu kitna pyaara hai."

Kuch lafz mit gaye hain, kuch yaad reh gaye,
Jo keh na sake hum, woh khat keh gaye,
Teri handwriting mein, ek sukoon sa tha,
Tu door thi magar, dil ke kareeb sa tha.

[Pre-Chorus]

Ab na koi paighaam hai, na tera intezaar,
Phir bhi kyun dil ko hai, tera hi khumaar,
Main khud se poochhta hoon, har raat tanha,
Kya tujhe bhi yaad aata hai woh guzra zamaana?

[Chorus]

Tere khat aaj bhi mere paas hain,
Inmein chhupe kuch adhoore ehsaas hain,
Tu toh chali gayi, mud ke dekha bhi nahi,
Par tere lafzon mein hum aaj bhi khaas hain...

Tere khat... tere khat...
Meri raaton ki aakhri baat hain,
Tu nahi, teri yaadein toh hain,
Mere paas bas tere hi khat hain...

[Verse 2]

Ek khat mein likha tha, "Kabhi chhodna nahi,"
Aaj sochta hoon, kyun kaha tha wahi?
Kya waqt badal gaya, ya badal gaye hum?
Ek hi shehar mein rehkar, ajnabi hue hum.

Woh baarish ki raatein, woh chai ki dukaan,
Tere saath guzra har ek woh armaan,
Ab baarish bhi hoti hai, par tu nahi aati,
Teri tarah koi bhi ab yaad nahi aati.

[Bridge]

Kaash ek baar tu saamne aa jaaye,
Jo dil mein hai, tujhe sab bata jaaye,
Na shikayat karunga, na sawaal karunga,
Bas tere khaton ka ek jawaab maangunga...

Tune aakhri khat mein likha tha,
"Khush rehna mere bina..."
Main aaj bhi koshish karta hoon,
Par khush rehna aaya hi nahi...
